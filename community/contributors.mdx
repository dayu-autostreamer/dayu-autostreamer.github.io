Dayu grows through code, documentation, translation, testing, research validation, review, issue triage,
and user support. Thank you to everyone who helps.

Commit history captures only part of the work. Helpful reviews, reproducible reports, research validation,
translations, and support also deserve recognition.
To suggest a correction or add missing credit, open an issue in the relevant repository with a link to the contribution.
Use [Support & Contact](./support.md) if you need help finding the right place.

## Origins and continued participation {#origins}

Dayu was founded by [Dislab](https://dislab.nju.edu.cn/) at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/).
We recognize that origin alongside each contributor's work. Contributors from other universities, companies,
and independent backgrounds are welcome to participate and take responsibility under the same [governance rules](./governance.md).

Historical contribution credit is retained when someone changes affiliation or steps back.
Start with the [contribution guide](./contributing.md) to add your own contribution.
