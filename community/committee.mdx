# Committee

Dayu's Technical Steering Committee (TSC) stewards project direction and governance.
Maintainers lead day-to-day technical work. These are separate responsibilities that one person may hold together.
The [system roster](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md) records project roles;
the [governance rules](./governance.md) describe how appointments and decisions work.

## Technical Steering Committee {#technical-steering-committee}

<CommunityMembers group="tsc" />

The Chair coordinates direction, governance, and community discussions. The Vice Chair supports agendas,
decision records, and follow-up, and can chair proceedings when eligible if the Chair is unavailable or recused.
These offices add no extra vote, technical approval authority, or repository permission.

## Maintainers {#maintainers}

<CommunityMembers group="maintainers" />

Maintainers share responsibility for technical quality, shared interfaces, compatibility, releases, and contributor
support across the system repository. They coordinate qualified review and help contributors find the right starting point.

## Contribute and take responsibility {#participate}

Anyone can contribute or provide a technically qualified review. To take on ongoing maintenance responsibility,
discuss your interest and contributions with a Maintainer and follow the
[appointment process](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#appointments-and-delegation).
Affiliation records a person's institution; eligibility and responsibility depend on individual contribution and judgment.

Visit [Contributors](./contributors.mdx) for broader contribution credit and [Contributing](./contributing.md)
to get started.

## Website maintenance {#website-maintenance}

The [website maintainer roster](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu-autostreamer.github.io/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md)
covers tutorials, translations, site code, and publishing. Its responsibilities are scoped to this website.
