# Dayu Community

Help build an open platform for cloud-edge stream analytics. We welcome people working on applications,
systems, research, documentation, and real-world deployments.

<CommunityPaths />

## Our community {#our-community}

Dayu was founded by [Dislab](https://dislab.nju.edu.cn/) at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/).
The laboratory's research and development are part of the project's origins. Our [committee](./committee.mdx)
and [contributors](./contributors.mdx) pages recognize the people behind the project and their affiliations.

Anyone can report an issue, improve a tutorial, review a proposal, validate research, or contribute code.
Responsibilities are earned through individual contributions and judgment. Institutional affiliation does not
confer a seat, a vote, or repository access. Read our [governance overview](./governance.md) to learn how we work.

## Find your starting point {#starting-point}

- **Use Dayu:** follow the [documentation](/docs/) and share questions or reproducible problems through [support](./support.md).
- **Improve the system:** contribute to [dayu-autostreamer/dayu](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu).
  Its development guides explain implementation details, shared interfaces, and tests.
- **Improve this website:** contribute tutorials, translations, navigation, and blog posts to
  [the website repository](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu-autostreamer.github.io).
- **Help others:** review changes, explain an issue, improve an example, or share a reproducible research result.

Our [contribution guide](./contributing.md) helps you choose a repository and prepare your first contribution.

## Research and citation {#research-and-citation}

Explore research and project updates in the [Dayu blog](/blog/). If Dayu supports your research,
use the [citation maintained in the system README](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu#citation).
For research collaboration, see [Support & Contact](./support.md#project-contact).

Read the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/dayu-autostreamer/dayu/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
