We are pleased to share that **Dayu has been published as an official case study on the KubeEdge website**. This milestone highlights how Dayu uses KubeEdge to support cloud-edge collaborative stream analytics.

<!-- truncate -->

Dayu is a framework for building and evaluating cloud-edge stream analytics systems. It provides a unified environment for organizing end-to-end analytics workflows, coordinating computing resources across cloud and edge nodes, and exploring scheduling strategies for real-world streaming applications.


Read the official Dayu case study:

- [English](https://kubeedge.io/case-studies/dayu/)
- [中文](https://kubeedge.io/zh/case-studies/dayu/)

We sincerely thank the KubeEdge maintainers and community for their review and support. We look forward to continuing our collaboration and sharing more cloud-edge stream analytics practices with both communities.
